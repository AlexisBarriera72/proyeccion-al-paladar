## What the company does

**Proyección al Paladar** presents itself as a gastronomic cinema experience in **Aguada, Puerto Rico**. Its Spanish profile sends visitors to Instagram direct messages for information about event dates. Its positioning combines movie screenings, themed dining and an immersive atmosphere. The statement that it is Puerto Rico's first such experience is the company's own claim; this research did not independently establish that distinction. [Official profile, S01](https://www.instagram.com/proyeccionalpaladarpr/).

## Payment and cancellation policy

The pinned policy post was published **September 10 2026 in Puerto Rico**, verified from the individual post's machine timestamp `2026-09-11T03:24:29Z`. Its graphic announces that **from November 15**, reservations require full payment, and cancellation with three days' advance notice returns 60% of the total payment. The year is not printed in the graphic. The research date precedes November 15 2026, so the announcement must not be treated as already active merely because it is pinned. [Policy post, A02 / E02 / S02](https://www.instagram.com/proyeccionalpaladarpr/p/DdIb7sxoOoR/).

The source does not settle the prior policy, exact cutoff counting, cancellations inside the window, refund processing fees/timing, rescheduling, no-shows or business-initiated cancellation. Top/bottom text is partially clipped in the supplied source graphic; no missing clauses are reconstructed. The prompt therefore requires owner-approved policy versions, effective dates and an accepted-policy record on each booking. It does not silently equate three days with exactly 72 hours.

## Recommended app model

The strongest supported direction is one **calendar and scheduled reservation app**: customers choose an experience, session and party size; staff manage dated showings, seats, manually received reservations and schedule changes. This is a product recommendation inferred from the observed fixed events. The appointment function should represent these reservations. A separate private-event consultation service remains optional and disabled until confirmed by the owner.

The detailed prompt specifies Spanish-first copy, optional English, **America/Puerto_Rico** venue times, mobile agenda/desktop calendar views, guest reservations, a protected staff workspace, party-seat accounting, duplicate-submit prevention, explicit inquiry/confirmed states, policy versioning and honest empty states. Sample records must be visibly marked DEMO and isolated from live bookings. Prices, sessions, address and capacity stay unset until approved. Payments and notifications cannot show success without actual verified integrations.

Reference patterns come from separate businesses: [Cinema Exquisito](https://www.cinemaexquisito.com/event-details/capitulo-dos-pulpfiction) for fixed movie/session/ticket selection, [CinePatio](https://cinepatio.com/) for a distinct private-event inquiry flow, [Sabor Arte Picnic](https://www.opentable.com/r/sabor-arte-picnic-san-juan) for showing inclusions before booking, and [Caribbean Cinemas VIP](https://caribbeancinemas.com/VIP/) for numbered seating only when a real seat map exists. None is affiliated with the target; no prices, menus, reviews or policies are imported from them.
