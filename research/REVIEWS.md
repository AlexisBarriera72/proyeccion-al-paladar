## Required evidence before enabling customer social proof

For each future review store: exact business match, source URL/permalink, platform, public author display name, published date if available, captured date, original language, concise excerpt or licensed full text, numeric rating only if explicitly present, and classification (`independent_review`, `company_curated_testimonial`, `customer_social_comment`, or `paid_or_promotional_content`). A comment complimenting a trailer without confirming a visit is not a verified dining review. Do not infer a date from relative UI labels or a star rating from positive words. Explain if a date or rating is unavailable.

Company-curated testimonial content should retain that label. Paid, gifted, or influencer promotional content should not be described as an independent customer review. Build a real moderation workflow, keep source provenance, and avoid presenting any locally entered feedback as verified solely because it was submitted through the app.
