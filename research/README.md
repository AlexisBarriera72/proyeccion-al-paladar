# Proyección al Paladar — research and app prompt

Prepared **8 October 2026** from the selected Instagram profile and public supporting research. This package is a research/build brief; an app has not been deployed and no booking, payment or customer message was sent.

## Start here

1. Extract the ZIP, then open **index.html** for the image gallery and document links.
2. Read **RESEARCH_REPORT.md** for the verified business findings and unresolved details.
3. Use **BUILD_PROMPT.md** as the detailed Sites build prompt. It includes the requested React, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, typography, animation, and anti-generic-design requirements. Give the builder this whole extracted folder so the source and image files remain available.
4. Use **IMAGE_MANIFEST.csv / .json** to choose images by ID, role, source, dimensions and usage limits. **CONTACT_SHEET.jpg** provides a quick visual overview.

## Important use notes

All images are captured references, with public reuse approval **unverified**. Company photos, customer likenesses and movie artwork need appropriate publication clearance; possession of a file does not establish it. Production images should come from approved owner originals or otherwise cleared assets.

Promotional posters are not live inventory. The November 15 payment/refund policy graphic omits its year. Current prices, exact address, capacity and policy application must be confirmed by the owner before accepting real reservations. The two Instagram recommendations are company-featured customer posts; sponsorship and verified-booking status are unknown, and neither supplies a star rating.

Scope: reviewed the 26 visible profile posts through captions and first images/reel covers, all three slides of the selected Shrek offer, two selected highlights, and targeted testimonial/policy detail pages. Full videos, every carousel slide, every highlight story, private messages and every customer comment are not archived. No generated imagery is presented as business photography.
